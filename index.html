Well, so this is a website I've made using Github Pages to increase awareness of ADHD!
So, what IS ADHD anyway?
Well, ADHD stands for Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder! This means that people affected by it have attention deficit and are sometimes quite hyperactive.
They also can have an array of other unpleasant symptoms such as impulsiveness, and poor time management. Chances are that you've seen someone with these symptoms.
